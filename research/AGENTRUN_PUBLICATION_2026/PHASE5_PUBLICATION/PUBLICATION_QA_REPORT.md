## Failures and open items

No gate failed. Four things are **not** claimed and are recorded here instead:

1. **The bibliography was not re-verified against publisher records in this session** (B5). Web search was
   unavailable; the entries are inherited from the earlier verification pass unchanged, and no new reference was
   added. Re-verification before submission is listed in `author_checklist.md`.
2. **The venue candidate set was not discovered by systematic search** (`VENUE_SELECTION.md`). Five candidates
   were chosen from knowledge and then each verified against its official pages. A better-fitting venue that did
   not occur to us would not have been found.
3. **COLM 2027 and AAAI-28 details are unverified** because they are not yet published; they are marked as such
   and support no recommendation.
4. **Author, affiliation, funding, acknowledgement and ethics fields are empty by design** and must be filled by
   the authors (`AUTHOR_INFORMATION.md`). Nothing was invented.
